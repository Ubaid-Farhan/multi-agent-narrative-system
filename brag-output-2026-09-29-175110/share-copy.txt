Multi-Agent Narrative System: AI characters that argue, bargain, cry, bribe and improvise their way through a live street scene. Every run is a new story. Type one sentence and the AI writes the whole cast. A Director agent runs the arc and invents a twist, a Reviewer agent rejects lines that aren't realistic, and the scene streams live in Roman Urdu or English, with a voice for every character.
