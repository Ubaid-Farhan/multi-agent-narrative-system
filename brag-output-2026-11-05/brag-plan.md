# Brag Plan: AI Narrative Engine

## What is this app?
An AI story engine where autonomous characters with their own goals and memories play out locally grounded scenes, guided by a Director and checked by a Reviewer.

## The angle
Treat one Karachi traffic collision as a tiny live drama: four people, four agendas, a Director choosing the next voice, and a Reviewer checking each turn. The player UI is the hero, with the real Rickshaw Accident scene art and character portraits.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Over the actual Karachi scene art, land: **ONE CRASH. FOUR AGENDAS.** The four character portraits arrive in quick succession.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Pick The Rickshaw Accident and Roman Urdu or English, then start a new story.
- Watch Saleem speak in the real player layout while the Director narration and character roster remain visible. Dialogue is marked as an illustrative sample.
- Show the Director selecting a speaker, the Reviewer checking realism, logic and repetition, and a context-aware twist entering the event stream.

## Outro / punchline
Reveal the saved run timeline and replay action, then land on the README line: **AI characters that argue, bargain, cry, bribe and improvise.** Close with AI Narrative Engine.

## User flow worth showing
Choose a scenario and language → start a live story → watch reviewed turns arrive → replay a completed run later.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: warm, cinematic Karachi street drama with a playful product-demo pace
- Interpretation: local and character-led, with energetic but readable UI reveals and a confident close.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, light business bed with restrained interface and reveal accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: begin under the first frame at 0.30 volume; short fade-in, lower relative to SFX, fade out over the final second.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset, estimated 114.84 BPM; strong cues at 4.23s, 10.54s and 12.65s. Aim the player reveal and twist around these cues; keep text holds readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; the UI itself supplies the movement.
- SFX posture: sparse, warm and motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: start-story click at 3.8s; soft twist reveal around 10.5s; restrained logo accent at 20.0s.
- Restraint rule: no sound under every text beat and no voiceover.

## Nine-question planning rubric
1. App: a live, multi-agent scene/story engine with player and admin panel.
2. Strongest claim: every run is a new story; characters have psychology, memory, goals and inventory.
3. Visual hook: illustrated Karachi street scene against the actual dark player shell.
4. UI to show: scenario start card, live dialogue with character portrait, Director narration, reviewer/event trace, and saved-run replay.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 22 seconds allows one legible end-to-end flow.
6. Tone: default; warm, cinematic local drama with a playful demo pace.
7. Audio: vol-9 light upbeat bed with three restrained sound accents.
8. Caption: One Karachi traffic jam. Four stubborn characters. A story engine that argues back.
9. Flow: scenario/language selection → live turn stream → saved run/replay.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The collision — 3s
Full-bleed actual Karachi scene image with a dark edge treatment; four actual character portraits land in a row. Headline: “ONE CRASH. FOUR AGENDAS.” Small location label: “Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi”.
Sequential/interaction: four cast portraits appear one by one, with a final hold.
Audio intent: establish momentum, not melodrama.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft reveal hit as the headline settles.
Music: warm upbeat bed begins immediately.
Transition mood: image-led push → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Start the story — 4s
Recreate the actual start card over the scene: The Rickshaw Accident; Roman Urdu / English selector; New story button. The button is clicked, followed by a “LIVE STORY” marker.
Sequential/interaction: cursor selects Roman Urdu, then clicks New story.
Audio intent: make the user action feel tactile.
Audio-coupled idea: one low-risk click on New story.
Music: continue under the UI.
Transition mood: button click match-cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Characters take the floor — 5s
Show the actual story-player composition: title and Karachi subtitle, Saleem portrait, an explicitly illustrative dialogue line, Director narration bar and four-character roster. Let one extra character enter the roster on the beat. Copy: “Every character brings their own goals and memory.”
Sequential/interaction: show the cast one at a time; hold the dialogue line long enough to read.
Audio intent: human, warm, lightly playful.
Audio-coupled idea: portrait/cast arrival accents are sparse; keep dialogue silent and readable.
Music: build gently.
Transition mood: pan from player to event trace → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — A story with a Director — 5s
Two real event-trace cards appear: “DIRECTOR → chooses who speaks next” then “REVIEWER → checks realism, logic, repetition”. An amber event strip marks “Context-aware twist”. Copy: “Turns stream live. The story keeps its state.”
Sequential/interaction: cards arrive in order; twist strip lands after them.
Audio intent: make the system feel alive and legible.
Audio-coupled idea: soft low-risk impact on the twist strip.
Music: hold steady; major twist reveal may align near 10.54s.
Transition mood: timeline wipes into saved runs → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Replay the argument — 5s
Show a saved run timeline with reviewed turns and a Replay button, over the same street scene. Copy: “Every story run is recorded. Completed runs can replay if the AI is down.” Finish with the source line: “AI characters that argue, bargain, cry, bribe and improvise.” Then AI Narrative Engine.
Sequential/interaction: timeline entries appear in sequence, then Replay button, then logo/title.
Audio intent: resolve with a confident, playful payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: one restrained logo accent; let the final line hold at least two seconds.
Music: fade out over the last second.
Transition mood: clean amber end card
